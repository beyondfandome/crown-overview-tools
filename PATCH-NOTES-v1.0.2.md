# Crown Overview Tools v1.0.2

## GM House Resource Control
- Added **Adjust House Gold / Food** to **GM: Armies, Tiles & Economy**.
- GM can select a player House and enter positive or negative Gold/Food adjustments.
- Positive values add resources; negative values remove resources.
- Manual removal is capped at the House's current stockpile, so Gold and Food cannot fall below 0.
- Every adjustment posts the House's before/after Gold and Food totals to chat for an audit trail.
