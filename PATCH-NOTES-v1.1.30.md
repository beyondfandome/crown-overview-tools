# Crown Overview Tools v1.1.30

## Economy balance

- Trading Hall (Market Tier III) now applies **3.0x** export value to both Primary and Secondary trade goods.
- Town Square remains 1.0x / 1.0x.
- Market Square remains 1.5x / 1.5x.
- Trading Hall changes from 2.5x / 2.5x to 3.0x / 3.0x.
- No flat income was added to the Market line.

This keeps Mines as the stronger general-purpose Gold building while making Trading Hall a more meaningful specialization for provinces with high-value exports.
