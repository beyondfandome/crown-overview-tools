# Crown Overview Tools v1.2.30 — GM Military Menu Consolidation

- Replaced the ten separate GM military operation buttons in the main Crown Overview sidebar with a single **GM Military Control Panel** entry.
- The control panel is a categorized two-column dashboard with three groups: Army Operations; Encounters & Interceptions; and Diplomacy & Recovery.
- All ten GM actions remain available: manage/transfer/split/command, spawn event/mercenary soldiers, army audit, pending army encounters, army encounter history, pending character interceptions, interception history, military relations, release armies following battle, and cancel pending muster.
- Real-time counts display pending army encounters, interceptions, and battle-locked armies.
- Existing player-facing **Manage Army** and **House Military Relations** buttons remain in the Player: Military section.
- No changes to combat formulas, interception conditions, army pathing, or upkeep rules.

## Test

Open the Crown map as GM, choose **GM Military Control Panel**, and launch each listed operation. Confirm the Player: Military manage-army control still works. Use a backup world for initial verification.
