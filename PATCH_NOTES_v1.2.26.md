# Crown Overview Tools v1.2.26 — House Military Relations and Army Encounter Logs

- Player: Military → House Military Relations chart. Each House independently marks each other player House Friendly, Neutral or Enemy; default Neutral.
- GM: Edit Military Relations enables authorized override for any House.
- Only mutually Friendly armies bypass prompts. Neutral encounters ask both sides to remain peaceful or declare hostility. Enemy encounters offer Engage or Withdraw/fallback, never auto-battle.
- GM: Army Encounter Log displays pending encounters and up to 400 historical meeting/outcome entries; completed records also generate GM-only chat whispers.
- Historical in-progress relation prompts are converted to the new chart behavior when the GM refreshes.
- Friendly status is directional for character interception: an army owner or province-lockdown owner marking a House Friendly permits those characters through without rolls. Existing levy/mercenary accounting and army management are preserved.
