# Crown Overview Tools v1.2.29 — Normal Movement Through Interceptions

- Remove the obsolete +1 lockdown movement surcharge; the automatic opposed Intrigue roll determines passage.
- Characters who win an Intrigue interception or are allowed through by the defender pay the ordinary tile cost (including road discounts), not an additional fortification/blockade surcharge.
- A character whom the defender stops remains in the previous province and pays one attempted movement point, unchanged.
- Friendly passage permissions now also suppress hostile fortification movement penalties for friendly visitors.
- Ordinary hostile fortification costs remain intact when no interception is involved.
- No changes to army encounters, levy economics, battle module API, or character interception log.

Static checks complete; still requires live Foundry multiplayer testing.
