# Crown Overview Tools v1.1.69

- Fixed an LOS information leak in province hover tooltips.
- `Player Owner` is no longer rendered for foreign provinces outside the current reveal mask.
- Unseen provinces no longer reveal local House, ruler, allegiance, buildings, economy, or other political details through hover.
- Harmless map information such as province name, region, terrain, movement cost, culture/religion, and public status markers remains hoverable.
- A player's own holdings remain politically identifiable to that player.
