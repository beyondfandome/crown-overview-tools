# Crown Overview Tools v1.2.31 — Private Siege and Marriage Messages

- Quick Siege outcome messages are whispered to the acting player and all GMs, rather than public chat.
- Direct province marriage attempt results are whispered to the attempting player and GMs.
- Player wedding scheduling, wedding-day reminders, missed weddings, postponements, completed ceremonies and dissolutions are whispered to both participating Houses and GMs. The public herald socket is not emitted for these private wedding events.
- Existing private marriage proposals, acceptances and ordinary diplomatic takeover privacy remain intact.
- Siege, marriage and wedding mechanical outcomes are unchanged.

**Test in Foundry:** Use at least two ordinary player accounts and a GM. Verify that other players cannot see the marriage/siege chat messages, while involved players and GMs can.
