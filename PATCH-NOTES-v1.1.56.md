# Crown Overview Tools v1.1.56

- Diligent construction preparation now persists until the next successful building/upgrade in that region consumes it; it no longer expires at round end.
- Build UI displays a playful **ALL BUILDINGS ON SALE!** banner while a Diligent sale is active.
- Building detail cards show the normal price struck through beside the discounted sale price.
- Engineer 10% remains passive and stacks with Diligent 25% for 35% off when the Engineer is the builder.
- The Diligent sale is consumed only after a project successfully starts.
