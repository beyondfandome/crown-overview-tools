# Crown Overview Tools — v0.6.31

## Military upkeep rebalance

Army upkeep per 500 troops per round:
- Mob: 1 Gold / 1 Food (unchanged, preserving affordable early levy warfare)
- Light Infantry: 2 Gold / 3 Food
- Spearmen: 2 Gold / 3 Food
- Archers: 3 Gold / 3 Food
- Heavy Infantry: 4 Gold / 4 Food
- Pikemen: 4 Gold / 4 Food
- Crossbowmen: 4 Gold / 4 Food
- Light Cavalry: 5 Gold / 5 Food
- Lancers: 6 Gold / 5 Food
- Heavy Cavalry: 8 Gold / 6 Food

Navy upkeep per 5 ships per round:
- Fishing / Conscripted Vessels: 2 Gold / 2 Food
- Longships: 3 Gold / 2 Food
- Galleys: 4 Gold / 3 Food
- War Galleys: 6 Gold / 4 Food
- Greatships: 8 Gold / 5 Food
- Dromonds: 10 Gold / 6 Food

The intent is to keep one-province early expansion viable with cheap Mob levies while making professional armies and serious battle fleets painful to leave mobilized for long campaigns.

Trade-good values from v0.6.30 are retained. Market-building redesign is not included in this patch.
