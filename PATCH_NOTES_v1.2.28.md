# Crown Overview Tools v1.2.28 — Interception Player Notification

- When a character loses an automatic Intrigue interception check, the GM records the pending decision and the **moving player** receives an informational popup immediately.
- The popup identifies the character, destination province, and intercepting army/lockdown, and explains that the attempted movement is logged and awaiting the defender or GM to resolve it.
- The movement remains uncommitted and costs nothing while pending; after a stop decision, the existing rule charges one movement point and leaves the character in the origin province. Allow continues movement.
- Reconnected/rebound pending interceptions also send the notice. Notices are limited to once per request per client, and the player receives a result toast after resolution.
- All v1.2.27 military encounter, House relations, GM tools, and troop accounting code retained.

Requires multiplayer Foundry testing.
