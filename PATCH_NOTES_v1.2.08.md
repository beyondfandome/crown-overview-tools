# v1.2.08 — Diplomatic Gifts

- Diplomacy now presents marriage independently from a two-step Gifts tier and allocation flow.
- Gift base equals the Statecraft requirement for the next development slot: 5/10/12/16. Cities cannot receive gifts.
- Multipliers 1/1.5/2/3/4 grant +1/+2/+3/+4/+5. No combined bonus cap.
- Gold + Food must meet required value; at least half the required value must be Gold.
- GM validates gifts and House balances on receipt, deducts the resources before rolling, and stores the bonus in roll audit.
- Marriage flag/core behavior retained from 1.2.07.
- Influence-to-Diplomacy modifier is pending approval and not part of this patch.
