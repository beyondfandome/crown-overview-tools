# Crown Overview Tools v0.6.19

- A tile with any Mine-line building (Mine, Deep Mine, or Mining Complex) cannot build/upgrade to Granary, Slaughterhouses, or Deep Sea Fishing Boats.
- The build menu disables conflicting choices and explains why they are locked.
- The rule is also enforced when the GM applies a build, so pending requests cannot bypass it.
- Reciprocal protection prevents starting/upgrading the Mine line on a tile that already contains one of those Tier 3 food buildings.
- Retains v0.6.18 movement defaults and food values.
