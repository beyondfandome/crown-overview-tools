# Crown Overview Tools v0.6.30

- Rebalanced all 105 trade goods to the new Tier 2-6 output scale.
- Tier 1 is intentionally unused; Bog Iron, Clay, and Reeds are Tier 2 (2G/0F).
- Wheat is Tier 5 (0G/5F); Rye is Tier 4 (0G/4F); Iron is Tier 4 (4G/0F).
- Tier 6 remains six singleton crown resources: Fireplums, Highland Cows, Fermented Crab, Unicorn Horn, Jewels, and Weirwood Bows.
- Tier 6 resources are pure 6G/0F or 0G/6F.
- Market building behavior is unchanged in this release pending final multiplier/upkeep balance decision.
