# v1.2.2 — Influence consolidation

Built from v1.2.21 (user-requested version label).

- Defending player House Influence applies −3 to +3 to diplomatic defense, not attacker roll.
- Active Social buildings contribute tier +1/+2/+3 to local diplomatic DC, stacking across lines.
- Both bonuses apply to static DC and opposed defender roll.
- Demolishing an active Social building costs controlling House 2/4/6 Influence (floored at 0).
- Town Hall line increases muster recovery from base 5% to 10/15/20%.
- Earlier one-time Social Influence grants, GM Influence adjustment, Godswood Archers and rerolls retained.
