# Crown Overview Tools v1.0.0

## Port Travel & Port Security
- Armies and characters can use the existing connected Port network for Port Crossing.
- Port Crossing remains the fast but dangerous alternative to safe fleet transport.
- Army Port Crossing retains crossing casualties: 1-5 safe, 6-7 lose 1d10%, 8-9 lose 20%, 10 lose 30%.
- Characters retain the dangerous-crossing Wound rule; a natural 10 automatically increments the character's Wounds in module data.
- Traveling to a Port controlled by another player now requires explicit permission before the crossing roll, movement expenditure, or teleport occurs.
- The destination player receives an Allow/Deny dialog identifying the requesting player, piece/army, army strength, origin, and destination.
- Denied or timed-out requests spend no movement and do not move the army/character.
- Approval applies only to that specific Port Travel attempt; it does not grant permanent access.
- If the destination owner is offline, the request falls back to an active GM. If neither is available, the crossing cannot proceed.
- Port approvals and denials are recorded in chat.
- Own-Port travel does not require approval.

## Retained 1.0 Economy & Warfare Rules
- Market line: no Market 100% primary / 50% secondary; Town Square 100%/100%; Market Square 150%/150%; Trading Hall 250%/250%.
- Building costs: 5G / 15G / 30G.
- Demolished buildings stop contributing income.
- Rebalanced trade goods and army/navy upkeep remain intact.
