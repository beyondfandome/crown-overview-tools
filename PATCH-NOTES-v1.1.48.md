# Crown Overview Tools v1.1.48

- Adds persistent **Cursed Holding** province state.
- Cursed holdings produce only 50% of all positive per-round income after ordinary economy/market/building calculations. Negative income/costs are not softened by the curse.
- Province highlight tooltip always shows a public spooky `☠ 𝕮𝖀𝕽𝕾𝕰𝕯 ☠` marker regardless of intelligence/detail access.
- Adds GM **Curse / Uncurse Holding** button for the selected land province.
- One-time migration starts these holdings cursed: Winterfell, Harrenhal, Casterly Rock, Starholdt, Coldwater, Hellholt, Pyke, The Arbor, Storm's End.
- Initial curses are written as persistent flags; lifting one later does not cause it to be automatically re-cursed on reload.
