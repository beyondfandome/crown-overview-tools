# v1.2.11 — Godswood Archer Capacity and Diplomacy Influence Reroll

- Godswood T1/T2/T3 adds 100/250/500 Archer specialist training capacity respectively. No free units, normal manpower cap and upkeep; highest tier only.
- A failed automated Diplomacy attempt offers its controlling player a single 1-Influence reroll. GM performs both rolls; second d20 is final. Gifts are not recharged and one action is used.
- Offers are timed out after 60 seconds if unanswered. House Influence is checked again by the GM before spending. Ledger stores a one-use key for that attempt.
- No automatic reroll yet for out-of-map Intrigue: this action currently has no built-in success/failure roll. Duels remain excluded.
- Does not include the remaining proposed defending-House Influence-to-DC and other Social building rule changes.

Live Foundry testing required.
