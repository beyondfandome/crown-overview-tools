# Crown Overview Tools v1.1.61

- Moved the Diligent construction action out of the crowded My Characters / Move menu.
- Diligent characters now get **Prepare the Way** directly in the Build / Upgrade screen.
- Prepare the Way spends the character action and creates the persistent 25% regional sale until the next successful project consumes it.
- Existing Diligent sales still show the ALL BUILDINGS ON SALE banner and price slash.
- GM use of a player character now records Diligent preparation for that character's player controller when available.
- Reduced player-side map suppression/hover/piece-tooltip polling frequency.
- Route tooltip now caches its current route signature and skips repeated pathfinding while the selected piece and hovered destination have not changed.
