# v1.2.09 — Diplomacy and Siege Briefings

- Shows culture, religion and sworn-allegiance considerations before a diplomatic attempt, without exposing numerical DCs.
- Uses the existing culture and religion comparison functions; no new roll modifiers.
- Shows qualitative siege conditions based on settlement, fortifications, stoneworks, manpower, engines, duration and foothold.
- Hides exact siege DCs from non-GM players; GM retains numeric breakdown.
- No changes to siege or diplomacy resolution, Influence, building benefits, or gift costs.
- Influence-based defensive DC and Influence-building DC remain proposed, not implemented in this release.
- If identities are not yet finalized by the GM, previews may be incomplete; GM resolution still determines the final modifiers.
