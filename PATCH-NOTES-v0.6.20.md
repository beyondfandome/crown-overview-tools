# Crown Overview Tools v0.6.20

- Resource building exclusivity now begins at Tier 1.
- Fields/Granaries, Mines, Pastures/Slaughterhouses, and Fishing Wharfs/Deep Sea Fishing Boats are mutually exclusive building lines on the same tile.
- Building one of these four lines locks the other three lines immediately.
- Existing buildings may still upgrade normally within their own line.
- The restriction is enforced both in the build menu (LOCKED) and again during build validation.
