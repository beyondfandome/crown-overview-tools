# v1.1.94 — One-click GM Marriage Removal

- In My Realm GM view, a Married non-capital holding now displays a separate **Remove Marriage** button next to **Edit Marriage**.
- Clicking it prompts for confirmation and clears the province marriage status and spouse House fields in both the worldTile and house flags.
- Does not change political owner, local House, buildings, character marriages, Influence, or Main Actions.
- Revalidates House ownership and capital status before writing.
- Reopen My Realm to refresh the display.
- No other outstanding movement, pause, hover-display fixes are included in this targeted patch.
