# Crown Overview Tools v1.2.34

- Quick Siege detailed results are whispered to the attacker, the defending province's owner and all GMs. Pitched-battle declaration details are also restricted to the attacking and defending forces' controllers and GMs.
- Completed player weddings receive a public names-only announcement. The participating Houses and GMs retain their private detailed ceremony result. Unsuccessful or incomplete weddings remain private.
- Diplomacy result notifications to participating players now include the final d20, final total, defending target and success/failure margin (including defender-favored ties). GM breakdown remains private.
- Marriage Diplomacy provincial checks also include a numerical margin in their private report.
- Retains v1.2.33 reinforcement and orphaned army hold fixes.
