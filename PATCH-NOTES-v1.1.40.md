# Crown Overview Tools v1.1.40

## GM Marriage Management
- Adds **Manage Marriage Alliances** to the GM Armies, Tiles & Economy section.
- Lists all active marriage alliances with realm, character, target House, province, and creation round.
- GMs can end an alliance after confirmation.
- Ending an alliance marks the ledger entry as ended and immediately frees its marriage slot.
- Historical records are preserved with who/when ended the alliance.
- Ending a marriage does not automatically reverse province control created by Marriage Diplomacy.
- The administrative confirmation card is GM-only.
