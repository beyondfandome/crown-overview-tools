# Crown Overview Tools v0.6.22

- Raids now divert the target tile's province Gold/Food income (applied against that tile's next collection, since economy is collected at round start) instead of subtracting from the defender's realm stockpile.
- Cavalry counts double for raid carrying capacity; mixed armies calculate infantry and cavalry separately.
- Opposing armies continue to hard-block raids, including a second validation at resolution.
- Added Trade / Send Resources: Gold/Food leave immediately and arrive when the round advances.
- Added Bank Loans: 1–30 Gold, 20% total interest, due in three rounds; one outstanding loan per House; taking one consumes a character action; Dishonorable characters are blocked; default adds 10% and extends one round.
- Added Demolish Building: removes an entire building line with no refund and frees its slot; blocked under enemy occupation.
- Build management is blocked while an opposing army occupies the tile.
- Locked building options retain explicit UI reasons.
- Stone Pit/Quarry balance is intentionally unchanged pending further design.
