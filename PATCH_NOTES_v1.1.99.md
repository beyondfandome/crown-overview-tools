# CoA v1.1.99 — Province statuses and marriage eligibility

- Province hover now publicly and independently shows **Capital**, **Married**, **Occupied**, and existing **Cursed** status when applicable. No coring character or “Cored by” wording is displayed. Multiple statuses coexist.
- Province-coring eligibility (both GM and player) ignores the character's personal `marriage`/spouse text. Active province assignments in the marriage ledger still exclude re-use; House cap remains 3. Player province coring still consumes Main Action and requires a roll.
- Player-to-player wedding marriage eligibility is unchanged.
- No migration or deletion of existing marriage assignments.
- Requires live Foundry testing.

- Heraldic hover plaques: gold **Capital**, burgundy **Married**, iron-grey **Occupied**; existing **Cursed** typography and styling untouched.
