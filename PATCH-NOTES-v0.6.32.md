# Crown Overview Tools v0.6.32

- Building costs increased from 4/12/24 Gold to 5/15/30 Gold for Tier I/II/III.
- Market buildings no longer generate flat Gold/Food income.
- Without a Market, primary trade goods produce 100% and secondary trade goods 50%.
- Town Square: primary and secondary trade goods produce 100%.
- Market Square: both trade goods produce 125%.
- Trading Hall: both trade goods produce 150%.
- Market multipliers stack with seasonal Market Forces category modifiers.
- Existing construction timing, Development slots, Statecraft requirements, and v0.6.31 military/naval upkeep remain unchanged.
